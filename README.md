# Displayoptify rewind (Wip)

## Welcome to displayoptify!
Displayoptify is a music player that meant to work on roblox executors!

## here you can:
- play music (crazy i know)
- auto scan any sound asset 
- play local files too!
- nothing else really, what did you expet from a music player?
- a lot more!

## Why Displayoptify?
Most players want to play music while playing games, also i am one of them. and a lot of players use only one screen, me too! so why not make a script for anyone to play music, save music and a lot more!
